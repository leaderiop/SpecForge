EvidenceCargo.toml:reqwest entry — reqwest 0.12.15 with default-features false and features rustls-tls, json, blocking, multipart - native-tls and default-tls compiled out. HttpRegistryClient builds one Client reused across calls, preserving pooling.

The workspace opts reqwest 0.12.15 out of all default features and selects only rustls-tls, json, blocking and multipart, so neither native-tls nor default-tls is ever compiled - the TLS stack is the pure-Rust rustls by explicit choice rather than by platform accident. HttpRegistryClient builds a single reqwest::blocking::Client in new() (with a builder-set 30s timeout) and reuses it across every call in fetch_versions, fetch, search, download_wasm and publish, preserving connection pooling and consistent timeout behavior.

Explain like I'm 10

Imagine two twins tidying the same notebook with different rules: one notices torn pages (import cycles), the other doesn't. Ask one twin and the notebook looks fine; ask the other and it's full of problems. We need one twin with one rulebook that both the editor and the watcher use.

Proposed solution. one shared analysis core; debounce task calls parse_and_update.

Explain like I'm 10

The computer compares version numbers like words in a dictionary, so '9' comes after '10' the way 'zebra' comes after 'apple' — your search page recommends the ancient 9.0.0 over the shiny 10.0.0. Sort by numbers, not spelling.

Proposed solution. Store a normalized semver sort key column (or compute MAX in Rust over parsed semver::Version like the client's pick_highest). Convert unwraps to map_err -> 500.

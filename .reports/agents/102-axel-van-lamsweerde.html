Explain like I'm 10

Every goal neatly breaks into sub-goals, and every sub-goal names the rule that keeps it true. But the 'what could go wrong' notebook lives on a different shelf and never points back at the goal it threatens. You cannot ask 'which risks endanger this goal?' and get an answer.

Proposed solution. Add an obstructs/threatens edge from failure_mode to feature or invariant (the schema already supports typed edges), and surface it in specforge trace so risk-to-goal queries work.

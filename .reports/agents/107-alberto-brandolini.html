The event model in spec/events/compilation.spec is what event storming should become: events carry channel names, typed payload blocks, and comments explaining consumer ordering — including the rarest and most valuable note, who deliberately does NOT consume ('resolve_use_imports is NOT a consumer here — it runs in Phase 2 after registries_populated') and why co-consumers are independent. This turns workshop sticky notes into computer-auditable data.

Sometimes the margin note says 'nobody gets a letter from this office.' Useful — but it's handwriting in the margin, not a field on the form. You can't ask the computer which behaviors send nothing; you have to read every page yourself.

Proposed solution. Promote produces/consumes (and their deliberate absence) into declared fields or a sidecar list so event-graph lints (spec/extensions/formal/event-graph-linting.spec exists) can check every command behavior either produces or is marked passive.

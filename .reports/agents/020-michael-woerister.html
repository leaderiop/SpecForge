It's like sweeping only the crumbs you spotted, then repainting the entire kitchen anyway. It works, but the clever part — only redoing what changed — is skipped, so every tiny edit costs a full rebuild.

Proposed solution. apply deltas to the live graph red-green style, keeping the existing verify_incremental cross-check.

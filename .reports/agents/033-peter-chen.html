Auditor review

Peter Chen: An ER model should say what it means — cardinality belongs to the relationship, not to field-type side effects. Today every relationship the kind-product can enumerate is drawn and defaults to many-to-many, so 1:1 and 1:N are unrepresentable. The data exists: honor infer_cardinality's None by not emitting, let SchemaEdgeType declare cardinality, and fix the doc naming a field that does not exist.

Proposed solution. Emit a relationship only when a concrete field with that edge_label exists (build.rs already computes source_field via infer_cardinality — skip rows where source_field is None), and consider introducing a declared-cardinality attribute on SchemaEdgeType so extensions can state 1:1/1:N.

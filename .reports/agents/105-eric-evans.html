Bounded-context leakage: formal-extension vocabulary lives in the project glossary of a project that does not declare formal

The company word list includes surgeon slang, but the company employs no surgeons. New hires read 'RPN' in the official glossary and assume it matters here; it belongs to a department this office hasn't hired. Either hire them (declare the extension) or move the words to that department's own list.

Proposed solution. Declare @specforge/formal in the root (see Parnas finding), or move formal terms into spec/extensions/formal/glossary content; I001 (unused glossary term) could be extended to flag terms whose owning extension is not installed.
